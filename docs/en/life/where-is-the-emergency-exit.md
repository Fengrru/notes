So where is the emergency exit?

Everyone says it'll be fine once you get into university.

But once I got in,

I found that the escape route out of high school wasn't university.

It was still just a can that had been processed on an assembly line for over a decade.

The only difference is being shipped from one factory to another.

Flawless on the outside,

but inside, rotten meat mixed with additives.

The processing plant forces my body inside,

yet it pays no attention to where the soul gets out.

They also say the emergency exit of university is grad school.

But I've found that the moment an exit sign is hung on a wall,

it becomes jammed tight,

shoulder to shoulder.

And is it possible

that the emergency exit has to be found by ourselves?

Perhaps it's not that we lack creativity,

but that for too long we haven't been allowed to create.

If we want to take back a part of ourselves from the assembly line,

maybe what we need is

a vessel we can express through.

And for me,

it's a video made of scripts and shots.

What about you?
