The people alive right now will be gone in a hundred years.

Your degrees, your income, your looks, other people's opinions…

None of it matters.

Everyone will disappear.

So…

Please make as many happy memories as you can.

Forget the things you hate.

Cherish this moment, right now.

Be happy.
